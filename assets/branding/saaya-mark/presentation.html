The shadow
you cast.

A hand-shadow puppet, mid-sentence, in a pool of light.

साया · saaya (Hindi/Urdu), noun: shadow; a protective presence that stays with you.

The idea

Your hand makes the shape. The shadow does the talking.

It's literally the name

Saaya means shadow. Shadow puppetry is how every child first brings a shape to life with their own hands. Here the human sets the intent and the shadow carries it out.

It speaks and acts

The mouth is open. That's the agent replying in the room, running a turn, using its computer. It's a character, and it's not a robot face.

It's always held

The forearm stays in frame. Every shadow needs a hand behind it, and that's the approval gate: agents do the work, and people stay in charge.

  1. 1
    Pool of light. The room: the shared space where people and agents meet. The shadow only shows up where there's light.
  2. 2
    The eye. A gap between the fingers, so you're looking at light through the shadow. It's the only "face" detail, and it comes from the hand, not from a screen.
  3. 3
    Three fingers, staggered. The fingertips and the light between them are what tell you it's a hand. At small sizes they make a clear notched silhouette.
  4. 4
    Thumb jaw, open. The talking-hand gesture everyone knows. Open means mid-turn.
  5. 5
    The forearm. It runs off the tile, so there's always a person attached. In the dark variant it fades into the dark outside the light.

Explored and dropped

Peeking shadow-pal. A dark blob with eyes behind a speech bubble. It read as a ghost, and in Hindi "kisi ka saaya" can also mean being haunted. That's the wrong meaning for a product people hand work to.

Shared shade. A dome sheltering a circle (human) and a square (agent). Good story, but at icon size it looked like a mushroom.

Turn-taking S. Two stacked chat bubbles forming an S. Too close to every chat app, and the S didn't read.
