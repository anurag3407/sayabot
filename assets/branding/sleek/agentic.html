Why it reads agentic

Three cues, zero clichés

Agency is motion plus intent plus a queue of work. Each cue below is isolated from the same live parameters — change a slider above and these update too.

cue 01 · execution

The comet sweep

The ring thickens toward its head, so the loop reads as continuous forward execution rather than a static circle. Tail thin, head committed.

cue 02 · intent

The active-turn halo

A breathing halo around the lamp token marks the turn that is acting right now — the agent's presence, not a face. It pulses once per turn.

cue 03 · autonomy

The incoming queue

Fading tokens seated in the gap are turns waiting their seat. Deterministic turn-taking made visible: one lamp lit, one voice at a time.
