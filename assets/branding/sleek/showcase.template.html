saaya (साया) means shadow — the companion that moves when you move and keeps working after you stop. These three marks take that idea seriously: no robot faces, no sparkles, no chat bubbles. Just geometry, one warm light, and the brand's own line — one lamp lit, one voice at a time.

The directions

Three marks, one idea

Each is built on a 256-unit grid from exact arcs and 45° cuts, drawn as filled paths — no live text, no filters. Switch the surface, the colourway and the view; every download returns exactly what you see.

The system

Colour, type, construction

The palette is already the product's: ember is the brand, lamp means "it's your turn", void and paper are the two surfaces. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Palette

Ember carries the largest fields; lamp is reserved for the turn token. One-colour and reversed cuts are provided for every mark.

Wordmark

SAYABOT is drawn as custom geometric monoline caps — outlined paths, not a font. Display face Anybody, body Space Grotesk. The symbol leads; the type stays quiet.

Construction

Symbols live on a 256×256 grid, 8-unit module. Circular geometry uses true arcs; every diagonal is an exact 45°. Toggle the grid on any mark above to inspect it.

Shelf test

Recommendation

A · Shadow S is the most ownable: it turns the name itself into a mark, and the 45° seam is a signature nobody else in the category has. It scales from a favicon to a wall and reads in one colour. C · Gateway is the safest at tiny sizes and the most distinctive solid silhouette — a strong pick if the favicon matters most. B · Turn Ring tells the product story best on the dark theme, though its lamp dot is quiet on white.

Masters, lockups and black / white / mono / favicon / app-icon variants are in assets/branding/sleek/final. Letterforms are constructed paths (no font licence needed for the logo).
