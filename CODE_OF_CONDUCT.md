# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Sayabot to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, including with people who are new to the project
- Disagreeing about the code without insulting the person
- Accepting feedback, and giving it in a way the other person can use
- Taking responsibility when a mistake affects someone else
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use and build Sayabot

Examples of behavior that is not welcome:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, trolling, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission
- Any other conduct which would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

This code applies in all Sayabot spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms run for the project, and when someone is representing Sayabot in a public forum. Representing the project includes using an official email, posting from an official account, or speaking at an event as a Sayabot maintainer.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for making clear what acceptable behavior is, and they will take fair action in response to anything they judge to be out of line. They can remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and they can ban someone from the community spaces for behavior they consider harmful.

Reports are reviewed and investigated, and the maintainers will reply in a way they judge appropriate to the circumstances. They will keep the reporter's identity private from the person the report is about, except where the reporter asks otherwise or the law requires disclosure.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may lose their responsibilities on the project.

## How to report

Email **sayalabs.studio@gmail.com**. Include what happened, where it happened, and any messages or links that show it. If you would rather not write it all down at once, say so, and we will follow up.

If your concern is a security vulnerability in the software, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead, at the same address.
